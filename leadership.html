Leadership.

Our Pastor

Faithful to the Word.
Committed to people.

Pastor Jason is devoted to faithfully preaching the Word of God and helping people develop a deeper, more meaningful relationship with Jesus Christ.

At the heart of his ministry is a desire to see lives transformed by the Gospel, families strengthened through biblical truth, and believers equipped to serve God with purpose, confidence, and joy. His preaching is rooted in Scripture, shaped by prayer, and centered on the life-changing message of Jesus Christ.

With a heart for discipleship and compassionate ministry, Pastor Jason is committed to meeting people wherever they are and helping them take their next step in faith. He believes the church should be a place where people from every background can experience the grace of God, discover hope in Christ, and grow within a loving community.

His leadership reflects a commitment to biblical integrity, servant leadership, and the spiritual growth of every generation. Under his guidance, Mount Moriah continues to be a church where people are welcomed, families are supported, gifts are developed, and faith is put into action.

Whether you are visiting for the first time or have been part of the Mount Moriah family for many years, Pastor Jason's prayer is that you will encounter the presence of God, be strengthened by His Word, and discover a place where you truly belong.
