The Mount Moriah App

Church in your pocket.

Daily scripture, this week's schedule, the latest sermons, upcoming events, and prayer requests, all in one place on your phone.

Download the App

Open it on your phone, then add it to your home screen and it works like any other app. Tap your phone below for the steps.

iPhone users, tap here for steps ›
  1. Tap Download the App above. It opens in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari, the square with the arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner. The Mount Moriah app now lives on your home screen.
Android users, tap here for steps ›
  1. Tap Download the App above. It opens in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu in the top corner of Chrome.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if you see it.
  4. Confirm, and the Mount Moriah app appears on your home screen.
